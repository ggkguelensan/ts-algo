## Decisions supported so far

- Fix packaging: verified in an isolated installed consumer on Node and Bun.
- Keep temporal/causal separation as a candidate: temporal-only reduces this import footprint, but its result materialization has costs.
- Do not select the current query prototype as a winner: Remeda is smaller in the simple pipeline fixture and literal-reference inference is incomplete.
- Keep direct loops and sorted arrays as first-class baselines: index and abstraction advantages depend on selectivity and result size.
- Defer all final scope decisions until the remaining mandatory business and memory/type-cost experiments are complete.
