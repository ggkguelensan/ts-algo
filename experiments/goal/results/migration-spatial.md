## Cost and accepted limits

The constructor resolves live entities once per occurrence, then calls each coordinate selector once. Historical direct Map construction reads entities from forEach without get; public Source deliberately has a resolver. Node construction overhead exceeds the relative gate in some rows (especially coincident points); it is accepted for the uniform source/context/live-entity contract and explicitly shown, not a claimed algorithm improvement. IDs-only sources can avoid owning entity stores.

Lazy within has fixed Source/generator overhead, visible for empty/small results, but avoids materializing whole areas before limit. Large canonical DTO results still pay business projection and sorting and can lose to native scans. The nearest algorithm is the same geometry/tree pruning without additional entity resolution during search; resolution/filter/DTO is included in the business timing. Different competitor radius/tie adaptations are included. There is no claim about nearest eligible business entity. Public memory and scaled declarations are checked separately; see the migration audit for their coverage and limitations.
